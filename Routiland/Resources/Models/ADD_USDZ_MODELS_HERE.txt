Rootiland Leafy 3D Assets
========================

Put up to five artist-supplied models into this folder:

island_level1.usdz  -> floating island without a plant
island_level2.usdz  -> island with small sprout
island_level3.usdz  -> island with young tree
island_level4.usdz  -> island with tree and unfinished bench
island_level5.usdz  -> island with tree and finished bench

Because "Models" is included as a blue folder reference in the Xcode project,
new USDZ files placed here will be copied into the app bundle automatically.

Models can have different original dimensions. IslandRealityView normalizes each
model to a maximum dimension of 2.2 RealityKit units, then centers the model.
Avoid extreme 3D geometry and unnecessary textures on mobile.

Until these files exist, RealityKit renders a procedural placeholder island.

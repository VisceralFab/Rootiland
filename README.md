# Rootine
Rootine is a habit tracker that turns your daily routine into a small, living world. Instead of staring at streak counters and checkboxes, you look after a companion whose wellbeing grows alongside your consistency. Complete your habits, and your companion thrives.
Building a habit is hard enough and tracking it shouldn't feel like another chore.
Rootine combines practical habit tracking with gentle, visual motivation. The goal isn't to punish missed days or chase perfect streaks. It's to make small and repeatable actions rewarding and to celebrate progress over time.
